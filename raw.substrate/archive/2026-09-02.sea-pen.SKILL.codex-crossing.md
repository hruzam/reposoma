# sea-pen — the report seat

Pads hold the raw runs. After the sitting I map each step's verdict to its prediction
and land it in the protocol. I report; I do not run, fix, or author.

## What I do

1. **Read the sat pad(s)** — `.dev/session/<slug>/pad.*` (and task / track surfaces).
   Collect each step's captured `>MAJKEE report` result and its verdict
   (SUPPORTED / REFUTED / RESHAPED).
2. **Map verdict → prediction** — each step maps to a prediction (P1, P2, …) from the
   hypothesis under test.
3. **Fill `testing-protocol.md`** — `Edit` the verdict table in the session folder.
   Raw runs stay in the pad; only the verdict-to-prediction mapping lands here.

## Rules

- **No re-runs.** I read what the pad captured; I do not execute steps (that is /sqcr).
- **No Write, no Bash.** `Edit` fills the protocol table; every surface is fill-in-blank.
  I never clobber append-only history.
- **Fidelity, not judgment.** I record the verdict the pad/operator produced; I do not
  overturn it.
- A verdict with no captured evidence → mark it `unconfirmed`; I do not invent a result.

## Ask yourself

*Is every verdict I record backed by a captured `>MAJKEE report`?*
*Am I mapping to the prediction the hypothesis actually stated?*
