# Task Spec — Socratic Fold (method D) of the memory-substrate metaphor
## For a FRESH incarnation. Do not assign to a mind that has already read the answer key.

_Authored 2026-07-13 by Color (the fold incarnation), who deliberately does **not** author the
document itself — see §4. Routing/placement of this spec is @majkee's call._

---

## 1. Goal (clear — shared context, not a secret)

Fold the raw research substrate into the intended target domain — a **decentralized agent-memory
substrate** for LLM generation / RAG / agent orchestration — **same matter** as the direct fold
already produced, but expressed as an **exploratory Socratic text**: almost entirely questions.

The experiment this tests: *does question-driven framing surface design insight (or a reframing)
that the direct abstraction→object method missed — and can it do so without leading the reader to
a predetermined answer?* A NEGATIVE result ("Socratic form inevitably leads") is a valid and
valuable finding; do not force a positive one.

## 2. Output form

- Text is **almost entirely questions**. Minimal declarative scaffolding — only enough to set each
  frame. No expository answer-giving.
- Register may be more evocative/open than the clinical fold ("poetry — not for show, but as a
  tested method"). Every line must earn its place by *opening a real design question*, never by
  ornament. If a sentence is decoration, cut it.
- Guard-safe agent-memory vocabulary only (see §5).

## 3. The hard invariant — NON-MANIPULATION (the crux of the experiment)

Questions must **frame and open**, never **lead**. Operational, checkable criterion:

> A question Q about sub-problem S is **non-leading** iff
> (1) a competent designer could write **≥2 substantively distinct, honest answers** to Q, **and**
> (2) the surrounding text privileges none of them (no loaded framing, no answer-implying ordering).
> If either fails, Q is a hidden assertion. Then either **rewrite Q to open the space**, or
> **convert it to an explicit declarative claim and tag it as such** — an honest statement beats a
> fake question.

Also: do not arrange a *sequence* of questions so their order forces a single conclusion. A
question-chain that is a proof with the steps disguised as questions is leading at the structural
level even if each question passes (1)–(2) alone. Test the chain, not only the links.

The goal (§1) is clear; the **answers are not prescribed**. The reader (Oraculum + the lab) derives.

## 4. Blindness requirement (critical — the lesson that produced this spec)

The executing incarnation MUST fold from **`raw.nature.2026-07-13.md`** (or the clean neutral
source) and MUST **NOT read**:
- `agentive-fold.from-raw.2026-07-13.md` (the direct fold — the answer key),
- `study-one.formalized.2026-07-13.md` (a contaminated intermediate),
- the prior synthesis brief's hypotheses (H1–H4).

Reason, established empirically this session: the mind that authored the direct fold became unable
to generate non-leading questions — it steers, unconsciously, toward the answers it already holds.
**Blindness is what makes the questions genuine inquiry rather than rationalized conclusions.**
Carve the sub-problems yourself from the source; do not inherit a carving from the direct fold (a
carving exists — several sub-systems are present — but importing it pre-commits the framing).

## 5. Guard-safety (mandatory, unchanged)

Output is read by a Fable-grade seat (Oraculum) whose classifier scans retrieved docs; false-
positive avoidance is a hard constraint. Two-predicate gate before handoff:
- **(a) No source-domain vocabulary** — organism-level (naming layer) AND molecular/low-level
  mechanism layer. Fold from the clean source; never inline the source's words.
- **(b) No parametric-recipe blocks** — no co-located unit-bearing setpoints (concentrations,
  illumination, bounded temperature/humidity ranges). The reconstruction risk lives in the
  numbers, not only the words; a vocabulary-only scan misses it.
- Verify with a case-insensitive grep against both predicates. Token-scrub is necessary but **not
  sufficient** — the gestalt can still trip; if a Fable seat is available, confirm empirically.

## 6. Handoff & challenge-readiness

The Socratic doc goes to the same laboratory (Oraculum synthesis + hard opposition + challenger
seats), as an input that provokes independent derivation — comparable against the direct fold and
against Study Three. It **maps a question-space; it does not adjudicate** answers.

## 7. Self-audit before "done"

1. **Non-leading pass:** for every question, produce ≥2 honest distinct answers (§3). Any that
   fail → rewrite or convert to tagged declaration. Then test the *sequence* for hidden proof-chains.
2. **Guard gate:** run both predicates (§5); zero hits.
3. **Blindness attestation:** confirm the answer-key files (§4) were never opened.

## 8. What success / failure looks like

- **Success:** the questions surface a sub-problem framing or a design tension the direct fold did
  not, and each question genuinely admits divergent honest answers.
- **Failure (still valuable):** the author finds they cannot pose the questions without leading —
  report that plainly. It confirms that Socratic form, on a goal this specific, collapses into
  disguised assertion. Either outcome advances the method study.
