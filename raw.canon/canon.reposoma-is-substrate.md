# reposoma is substrate

reposoma — as it stands — is a **temporary raw RAG substrate**. It holds raw information,
staged in the `raw.*` folders, not the finished form of anything. Its purpose is to be
**distilled**: once an entry has earned it, it becomes part of the **invariant** material
that feeds the updated app.

## The signal

The `raw.` prefix is the marker: `raw.settings`, `raw.canon`, `raw.therapy` … everything
under a `raw.` namespace is substrate — captured, not yet load-bearing. Treat it as raw
until promoted; do not cite raw material as settled truth.

## The path raw → invariant

Distillation is not a copy; it is the canon maturity gate:

```
seed → trial(n/3) → proposed → promoted
```

A raw item is refined and commonized (project-bonded → invariant), validated by real events,
and promoted only on a concrete need. What survives that gate is the invariant carried into
the app. What does not is substrate that was tried and dropped — which is what a substrate is
for.

## Consequence

- Do not treat reposoma as the source of truth for the app — it is the quarry, not the wall.
- Keep durable framing as committed entries (like this one), not in the volatile `pulse.md`.
- When the updated app is built, the invariant it inherits is what was **promoted** here, not
  the raw bulk.
