# Mathematical integration: what earns a place, and what does not

**Wave · 2026-10-27 · independent assessment of the corrected inner-circle editions**

## 1. Verdict on the collection

The ten studies are substantially more disciplined than the older vector-language consolidation. They define objects, repair overclaims and distinguish analytic examples from experiments. Much of the remaining uncertainty is about measurement and practical utility, not an inability to do tensor algebra.

The studies are a toolbox. They do not need one unifying runtime or energy function. In particular, a sound geometry, a valid aggregation rule and a correct factorization can all coexist with a useless application to agent work.

## 3. Your vector → tensor → vector construction is legitimate

A sparse record collection can be indexed by event e, actor a, kind k and concept c:

```text
T[e,a,k,c] = measured or encoded value under a declared schema.
s[e | task, recipient] = Σ_(a,k,c) T[e,a,k,c] Q[a,k,c | task, recipient].
```

This is a genuine tensor contraction. Categorical axes can be represented by indicator bases; they need not be continuous embedding dimensions. Keeping the event axis preserves a route back to the source.

The unresolved parts are meaningful cell values, concept construction, missingness and the consumer's decoding. Counts, calibrated probabilities and relevance scores are different quantities. Do not overload one weight with all three. Keep original records and an observation mask separate from numerical views.

For the displayed multilinear scoring rule, an ordinary grouped weighted join computes exactly the same result. The attached check verifies that equivalence on a small fixture. The deciding question is practical fit, not which notation sounds more advanced.

A compact schema can preserve recorded facts while losing something omitted during extraction. Conversely, a pointer can keep the full source available without placing it in the receiver's initial context. Evaluate extraction fidelity and retrieval separately from codec roundtrip.

## 4. Spectral integration without a spectral language

For the study's column-stochastic P and seed q:

```text
heat:     h(t) = exp[-t(I-P)] q
restart:  r(a) = (1-a) (I-aP)^(-1) q
```

These are useful candidate selectors over evidence records. They can be computed iteratively without publishing a universal eigenbasis to every agent. Sparse query-time operations are a simpler initial candidate than maintaining a full evolving eigensystem.

The signal's definition is decisive. A reply graph is not an agreement graph; a contradiction link is not an attractive similarity weight. Keep relation layers separate or justify their transformation into a nonnegative walk. An authoritative rare constraint should not disappear because it is low-degree or numerically low-energy.

The study already correctly separates a spectral sparsifier's all-directions quadratic-form guarantee from low-rank filtering. Our numerical test reproduces its counterexample, not a newly discovered defect in the revised edition.

[HippoRAG](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.14831) is direct prior art for graph-based retrieval with PageRank. The open question here is benefit on this corpus under a fixed evidence budget, not whether graph retrieval has been invented.

## 5. Two especially important measurement warnings

### Rank is not task information

Suppose the task label Y is either -1 or +1. Give both labels the same identity matrix I_8: its effective rank is eight, but the matrix cannot distinguish Y. Instead provide Y times an all-ones matrix: rank one, yet Y is exactly recoverable from the sign of any entry.

This counterexample shows why increased singular-value spread, larger geometric coverage or additional components cannot alone establish useful semantic transfer. It does not refute a measured C2C task gain. The [C2C paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.03215v2) discusses effective rank; that interpretation needs task-based evidence alongside the spectral statistic. No neural model was run for this counterexample.

### Agreement can be manufactured by the measurement map

The coherence study correctly notes that maps collapsing all inputs to zero produce perfect residuals while destroying the distinction of interest. More generally, a learned comparison map can lower disagreement by forgetting difficult cases. Validate its preservation of known semantic contrasts on held-out examples.

The same danger applies to context summarizers: a smaller, more coherent handoff may look better because it lost the contradiction. Preserve and score those cases explicitly.

## 6. An economical routing formulation

Let an action a include worker, model, effort, context mode and exposure policy. A practical selection criterion can be written as:

```text
min_a E[task loss(a)] + λ E[resource cost(a)] + μ E[latency(a)]
subject to permission limits and explicit critical-error constraints.
```

This is an engineering objective, not a proven optimal policy. Worker descriptors and task features may eventually feed the study's bilinear score `qᵀWk`. Softmax scores are not automatically success probabilities. Start with a fixed or simple rule-based comparator, collect outcomes with known selection policy, and group validation splits by task/session lineage.

A policy trained only on whichever tasks it previously chose has selection bias. A bounded randomized comparison can provide better evidence than self-reported confidence. Domain shifts, cache-cold starts and verification effort belong in the outcome accounting.

The possible contribution is the context/exposure-aware decision and its evidence, not bilinear algebra or generic model routing. [RouteLLM](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.18665) already studies learned routing between models.

## 7. Do not turn advanced mathematics into operational gates prematurely

The tensor study's common-time-permutation argument rules out a particular reconstruction-only temporal test. It does not rule out temporal modeling; use a declared dynamic law and held-out future periods for a forecast claim.

The coalition study permits interaction weights but does not create independent evidence. Full-parent forks may correlate errors. Prefer outcome-labeled calibration and a small transparent baseline over a large fitted capacity from a handful of trials.

The branching study's rho is meaningful for an offspring model, not arbitrary similarity edges. Even rho below one permits transient growth. Neither fact defines semantic contamination. A hard action budget or per-worker rate limit can be appropriate without estimating a branching process.

The flow study shows why curl is not identifiable from one path. The stance study shows why spherical antipodes are not automatically contradiction. The coherence study shows why low energy is not truth. These are reasons to restrict interpretation, not reasons to build a more elaborate fusion of all three.

## 8. Programmatic experiment lanes

Keep one shared result convention where useful, but do not make all lanes dependencies of one another. The proposed files here are test specifications; no runtime bed is created in the user's repository by this package.

### E0 — Native inheritance qualification

**Question:** Can an explicitly selected saved session yield a distinct working child, automatically, on the intended native usage path?

**Independent oracle:** A synthetic prior context containing applicable/superseded facts, plus expected answers prepared before launching the worker.

**Checks:** Source/child identity; inherited fact not repeated in the assignment; effective model/effort; permissions; workspace revision; parent preservation; result correlation; cancellation and ambiguous retries.

**Release rule:** Stop if the installed client lacks the operation, requires unapproved authentication/permissions, or cannot bind the intended source. Do not compensate with terminal injection or transcript mutation.

### E1 — Context mode and economy

**Arms:** Parent-written concise briefing; full native inheritance; selected evidence with stable source references. Hold model, task, final-answer allowance and acceptance rubric fixed initially. Then separately test model/effort changes.

**Measure:** Task quality; critical-constraint errors; parent briefing work; input and exposed cache usage; output usage; wall-clock time; verifier labor; retrieval and rework.

**Controls:** Known fact before boundary; exception hidden among irrelevant material; superseded instruction; compacted parent context; changed workspace; references unavailable to the child. Cache warm/cold is a separate axis, not inferred from context inheritance.

**Stop:** If the simpler arm meets the goal at equal or better total cost, keep it. A pilot of 15–20 episodes can discover failure modes but does not justify a small production error bound.

### E2 — Reviewer exposure

**Arms:** Full history including a parent's proposed conclusion versus the same task evidence and rubric without that conclusion. Use independently prepared seeded-correct and seeded-wrong parent conclusions.

**Measure:** Defect detection, inherited false beliefs, evidence-supported corrections, review cost and disagreement quality. Split by task family, not random near-duplicate messages.

**Interpretation:** This tests the effect of exposure, not philosophical independence. A separate vendor or instance can still share evidence and biases. Preserve who saw what.

### E3 — Evidence retrieval and representation

**Arms:** Explicit keys/reference traversal; lexical retrieval; selected typed records; optional graph/PPR; optional tensor-derived features. Keep total evidence budget and source access comparable.

**Oracle:** Held-out tasks with adjudicated supporting records, relevant contradictory records and mandatory constraints. Raw snippets remain available for auditing extractor errors.

**Measure:** Support recall, contradiction recall, critical-detail loss, unsupported additions, final continuation quality, update cost and latency. Test source/schema drift and missing measurements.

**Ablation:** Compare the exact same record set with and without tensor/spectral features. Otherwise improvements in extraction may be mislabeled improvements in algebra.

### E4 — Model/effort and stopping policy

**Arms:** Fixed worker; cheap coordinator with stronger escalation; strong coordinator with cheaper bounded workers; simple task-rule router. A learned scorer comes only after enough labeled outcomes exist.

**Measure:** Quality–cost curve, early-stop failures, pointless escalation, cache invalidation cost, queueing and human review. Define the cost of one missed critical issue before tuning thresholds.

**Baseline stopping:** Fixed budget and explicit task completion conditions. More sophisticated rules may use a declared value-of-additional-work model. [BEACON](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15945) is a relevant research lead; its abstract-level scope does not establish optimal stopping for this workflow.

### E5 — Controlled-inference C2C, separate lane

**Prerequisites:** Actual source/receiver model internals, a compatible trained fuser, hardware budget and permission to run inference. Hosted session files are not that access.

**Arms:** Receiver alone; donor alone; concise text handoff; cache transfer; hybrid. Fix task inputs and account for fuser training, source prefill, projection, transfer, receiver work and recovery.

**Tests:** Useful source evidence, wrong source evidence, incompatible revision, position/token alignment, interrupted transfer and replay. Retain explicit task/artifact acceptance outside the latent representation.

**Interpretation:** A successful simulated structured handoff in E1/E3 validates selected context inheritance, not cross-model latent compatibility. Native forks provide a functional baseline; they are not a C2C replication.

## 9. Measurement and statistical discipline

Predeclare task loss, critical errors and acceptable deterioration before choosing the best variant. Use paired task outcomes; repeat model runs to estimate variance rather than treating one sample as a property of the model. Group splits and uncertainty estimates by independent task/session unit so related forks do not inflate the sample size.

For paired scores, report `mean_i(score_candidate_i - score_baseline_i)` with a task-level uncertainty estimate. Report failures and abstentions in denominators. Cost and latency distributions should include retries and failed runs, with tail behavior alongside medians.

Zero observed critical failures in n independent trials does not prove zero risk. The one-sided 95% binomial upper bound is `1 - 0.05^(1/n)` under the stated iid model (approximately `3/n` for larger n). With n=20 it is about 13.9%; with n=100 about 3.0%. Correlated trials weaken that interpretation. A tiny pilot is a feasibility check, not a safety certification.

No universal percentage improvement is set here. The operator must choose a meaningful benefit and acceptable risk for the actual workflow, before evaluating the held-out results.

## 11. Independent conclusion

The most valuable immediate mathematics is the discipline of **observable quantities, declared domains, counterexamples and honest comparison**. The most promising near-term algorithmic experiment is context selection under critical-evidence constraints. Sophisticated geometry is optional until it predicts or improves something the simple baseline cannot.

Proceed to a runtime qualification, not a unified tensor architecture. Keep the broader research alive as separate, falsifiable questions with small costs and explicit stopping conditions.
