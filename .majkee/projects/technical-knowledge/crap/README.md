# Rejected claims and low-value noise

myShell judgment, 4 November 2026. “Crap” here applies to the identified claim or
to its lack of reusable content. A useful underlying paper or tool can remain in
the useful pile.

| Captured claim | Why it is rejected | What survives |
|---|---|---|
| TC05: near-zero retriever hallucinations means zero liability | The reported metric is invalid document identifiers; it does not establish correctness of final answers or a liability conclusion. | Structure-aware retrieval and separate error measures. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.03874v2). |
| TC09: 109M beats 72B, therefore model scale is generally defeated | Representation-alignment results do not establish general assistant superiority. | Study the task and paired-view assumptions. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39302). |
| TC18: the title makes these official Anthropic skills | The repository explicitly says it is independent and unaffiliated. | Community content with its actual owner. [Notice](https://github.com/mukul975/Anthropic-Cybersecurity-Skills). |
| TC53: a video-variation workflow guarantees huge view counts | A repository and demonstration do not establish audience outcomes. | A tool lead, parked pending a real use case. |
| TC13: salary stories or a “10x” headline establish reliability | They provide no usable comparison protocol for this workspace. | Explicit progress, recovery and verification mechanisms. |
| TC01: a short transformer animation is a recipe for building a production assistant | The illustration omits training, data and evaluation requirements. | Attention as a learnable mechanism. |
| TC31: LRU must always beat FIFO | A small counterexample is sufficient to reject the universal claim. | Compare policies on a workload. [Verified illustrative trace](../../cache-methods/application-caches.md). |

The final row rejects an overgeneralization one could take from the reel; it is
not a quotation of a claim that the author explicitly stated.
