# Agent workflows

TC13–TC21; extracted 4 November 2026. Product names remain leads, with their
individual evidence status in the [catalog](../../technical-clippings/CATALOG.md).

## Continuity should be reconstructible

Anthropic's long-running-agent article describes an initializer, incremental
coding work, progress records and checks that help later sessions recover context.
[Primary article](https://www.anthropic.com/research/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents).

My transferable lesson is to keep a small factual record of completed work,
unfinished work and verification. A later session should be able to check those
claims against artifacts. The captured workshop's salary, speed and reliability
headlines add no necessary knowledge to that practice.

## New memory infrastructure needs a measured gap

Browser Use, scientific skills and OpenViking are concrete project leads from the
initial source check. Their existence does not establish that this workspace needs
them. My evaluation questions are: what current failure would this dependency fix,
what data would it retain or transmit, how is a bad result detected, and what is
the cost of maintaining it? Keep those questions beside any future experiment.

TC18's cybersecurity skills collection is community-created and explicitly
unaffiliated with Anthropic. Use its actual owner, not the title, as provenance.
[Repository notice](https://github.com/mukul975/Anthropic-Cybersecurity-Skills).

## Curation can be an engineering outcome

Karpathy's HackerTube post describes filtering recommendations into a focused
catalog with portable data. It is a useful example of reducing an information
source to something reusable. It is still one author's project account, not a
general delivery-time guarantee.
[Author's account](https://karpathy.bearblog.dev/hacker-tube/).

My reuse here is the same pattern: claims become notes, notes get sources and
dispositions, and only a small queue remains active. The diagram collection and
Awesome Harness Engineering source identities remain unresolved in the
[unproven pile](../unproven/README.md).
