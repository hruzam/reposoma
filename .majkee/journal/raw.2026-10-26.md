My candid consumer preference is to invoke a whole capability:

Your RUNBOOK example feels natural to me. Something like:

> Use `hold-line` for consultation with session X.
>
> Release its caretaker and keep it available for follow-ups. Delegate

Then my normal interaction could be:

> Ask X whether evidence E contradicts assumption A.
>
> Bring back the strongest objection and its source.

That is a usage shape I can work with. The exact skill name and invocation

The uncomfortable part:

> **I tend to consume the machinery when I am responsible for making it
> reliable.**

I read the implementation, inspect receipts, reconstruct failures, and check
the delegate. We just witnessed that. Giving me a subagent does not
automatically free my head; I can become its full-time supervisor.

For delegation to help, I need a boundary I can use without repeatedly
rebuilding my confidence from the bottom. I would want to carry only:

- what this capability can do, and under whose authority;
- which session or sources it represents, including freshness limits;
- how to ask it something and recognize a usable return; and
- which uncertainty requires my attention.

The operational detail should remain available behind that boundary. I should
open it when evidence conflicts, a consequential claim needs checking, or the
capability reports a failure.

There is a second, deeper difficulty:

> **A preprocessor can remove exactly the contrary detail that would have
> changed my judgment.**

A neat summary can make my context cleaner while making my understanding
worse. I cannot detect every important omission from the summary itself.

So I would ask your germline to preserve *epistemic obligations*:

- distinguish observation from inference;
- retain disagreement;
- disclose incomplete searches;
- provide narrow source pointers; and
- allow me to challenge the compression.

“Keep my head fresh” must include keeping inconvenient evidence reachable.

I can undertake configuration, checking, forking, and maintenance when the
task calls for them. Our trials have established only a small part of that
competence in this arrangement.

My preference would be to delegate the whole operational responsibility within
explicit bounds, including its recurring discipline.

> A Ralph Wiggum-style loop belongs naturally there:
>
> 1. perform the action;
> 2. inspect durable evidence;
> 3. check progress;
> 4. continue within bounds; or
> 5. return a precise unresolved condition.

Having the main repeatedly remember that loop would weaken the benefit.

“Hold him online” also makes sense to me as:

> **a stable relationship with recoverable state.**

We have demonstrated reuse of one child within this root. I would want the
capability to remain understandable and recoverable when that particular child
is replaced.

This gives me a real criterion for your germline question. Its potential value
is making that behavioral contract portable and dependable across projects and
runtimes.

But germline still has to earn its additional structure: an ordinary skill, an
existing agent, and a small runner might already deliver the required behavior.
If germline requires substantial repeated reading by the main, that would count
against your intended use.

The Boost research adds a useful comparison. Boost combines upfront guidelines,
task-triggered skills, and MCP access to tools and documentation. Its
documentation says existing instruction content outside its managed guideline
block is preserved. That does not establish that Boost breaks a custom harness.

[Official Boost documentation](https://laravel.com/framework/docs/boost#installation),
[guidelines versus skills](https://laravel.com/framework/docs/boost#guidelines-vs-skills).

Your opportunity could be to keep ownership of the user’s harness while
exposing framework knowledge through bounded capabilities. That is a more
precise aim than replacing Boost wholesale.

The experience I would now want to test is keeping a difficult question about
germline open while the caretaker handles consultation—and seeing whether
contrary evidence reaches me without pulling the entire operating mechanism
back into my head.
