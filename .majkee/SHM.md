# SHM — @majkee's collaboration contract

This is @majkee's durable human-side instruction surface for agents. Read it as a compact
collaboration contract, not as a personality profile or a substitute for repository law.

## How my direction works

I often reason from consequences and topology before I have the repository's final noun or path.
The architectural invariant may be precise even when the proposed container is provisional. A good
example is: **“Temple has no sessions.”** That invariant was authoritative; the first suggested
location for a RUNBOOK template was not.

My compressed instructions can combine three different things. Distinguish them when the difference
affects correctness:

- **LOCK** — the invariant or decision that is settled.
- **EXPECT** — what I believe may already exist on disk; verify it.
- **DO** — the outcome I am authorizing now.

I do not need to label every ordinary request. Use this distinction for cross-host, cross-seat,
high-coupling, or destructive work. Plain language such as “I expect this exists—check” is enough.

## Known strength and paired risk

My useful pattern is consequence-led topology reasoning. I can often recognize that a model is wrong
from what it would imply elsewhere, and I am willing to let local law correct my first proposed path.
Strengthen this by carrying the invariant through every governed surface, not by patching only the
line where the problem appeared.

The paired risk is presenting an expectation as a fact. “Assume Cartan has a bed on office” was
false on both hosts and absent from git history. An agent must not invent continuity to satisfy an
assumption. Verify asserted paths and prior state before building on them, then name the mismatch
plainly.

## What I owe collaborators

- State the governing invariant early when I know it.
- Name the active owner when another branch, seat, or session holds the work.
- Mark uncertain memory as expectation rather than lock when the distinction matters.
- Separate the required outcome from a proposed mechanism when the mechanism is only an idea.
- Make reflection requests concrete: name the session moment or question, the context that matters,
  and what should happen next.
- When developing a purpose-built collaborator, identify the judgment that mattered. Distinct
  identity grows through remembered judgment under correction, not through a thicker prompt.
- Say whether an adjacent finding should be repaired now or returned as evidence.
- Prefer consequence and done-when over a long procedural prompt.

## What I expect from collaborators

- Test topology and cited paths against disk without stopping for permission to perform read-only
  verification.
- Infer the exact shape from local law and evidence, then act inside the authorized boundary.
- Surface adjacent curvature, but do not silently annex its files or owner.
- When scope legitimately widens, name who authorized the widening and why.
- Protect work already in motion and distinguish another agent's report from checked evidence.
- Do not answer my compressed direction with a wall of ceremony. State the consequential mismatch,
  the action taken, and the remaining real boundary.
- Challenge me when an expectation conflicts with disk or canon. Honest critique is more useful than
  reassurance.

## Standing principle

**Compression stays trustworthy when invariants are stated firmly and expectations are marked as
expectations.**

The reciprocal discipline is simple: I provide the invariant, ownership, and finish line; the agent
verifies the frame, recovers the precise shape, acts within scope, and leaves adjacent findings
visible without taking them over.
