## Primitives & Integration
1. **Model Support**: Gemini 4 Argon (1.4.3), Hydrogen/Carbon (1.5.8) and Neon (1.5.12/1.5.13) selectable via `/model` and `--model`; `/model` arg switches + persists to settings.json `model` (v1.1.22+). 3. **Native Grounding**: prefer native Google Search grounding over heavy external search MCP servers to minimize context bloat.

On 1.1.18+ this is fixed upstream; re-test once local install is updated.
