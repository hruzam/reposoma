## 2026-07-13

**Lead:** Mycelium is an active, adaptive redistribution network — not a passive conduit. The same hyphal strand simultaneously performs structural, transport, enzymatic, and electrical signaling functions; anastomotic looping (not branching trees) is the architecture that makes concurrent function possible. At global scale, terrestrial plants allocate ~13.12 Gt CO2e/year to mycorrhizal mycelium (~36% of annual fossil fuel emissions). Largest known organism: Armillaria ostoyae (Oregon), 2,200 acres, ~8,000 years old, ~31,500 tonnes; persistence achieved through quiescent tip meristemy and extreme genomic stability (only ~163 genetic variants across 75 ha over 2,500 years). Electrical signal propagation confirmed plant-to-plant at ~0.5 mm/s via fungal bridges; functional role remains experimentally unresolved.
