# src/ — the vacuole (meeting scenarios still cooking)

The **vacuole** is the contained room where meeting scenarios are RUN and OBSERVED
before any finding earns a `res/` chapter. Nothing here is doctrine — it is experiment.
A scenario graduates ("closes from the vacuole") into `res/` only when it *repeatedly*
proves its finding.

## Scenario #1 — CAPTURE (open · highest priority)
- **Problem:** chatbot meetings capture as voice → lossy manual relay → self-reported
  debriefs, not ground-truth transcripts. Every diagnostic (concession log, floor-share,
  last-word, who-was-right) is impressionistic until capture is solved.
- **Hypothesis:** seat a **scribe** — Ptyra REGIME B · STENOGRAM — whose only job is the
  transcript, turning self-report into a measurable record.
- **Test:** run a short meeting with a scribe seat; compare the scribe's log against the
  two voices' debriefs; does it make "who won" falsifiable?
- **Graduates to:** `res/capture.md` when it works.

## Scenario #2 — "untested?" anti-convergence move (open)
- Any voice or the chair may call **"untested?"** to force one round of *"what have we
  both left unchallenged?"*
- **Test:** does it repeatedly expose a missed assumption? (v1 did this once by accident,
  and it was the most useful minute.)
- **Graduates to:** `res/anti-convergence.md` if it repeatedly earns corrections.
