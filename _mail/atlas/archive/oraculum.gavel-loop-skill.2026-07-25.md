## What to build

Global skill `~/.claude/skills/gavel-loop/SKILL.md` — a sequential gavel-clearing
protocol for working down a queue of ungaveled decisions with an operator, one decision
per loop. Proven live: nabla-lab 2026-07-24/25, 8 locks + 15 days of ledger debt cleared
from a mobile phone with one-line operator stamps.

## The loop shape (core mechanic)

Per gavel, the seat presents exactly four parts, then stops:
1. **Impact** — project-coding consequence · reversibility · reach · crucialness
2. **Wider context** — what happened, where the item came from (origin story, grounded
   in artifacts, thin-read/grep first)
3. **Seat lean** — one recommendation with reasoning; attribution explicit when the lean
   relays a research output rather than seat judgment
4. **Stamp** — operator: gavel / amend / park. Amendments fold verbatim-faithful into
   lock text; operator wording wins over seat elegance.

## Class protocol (pre-classification pass, runs once before looping)

- **Formal** items → blessed-by-default, logged in a visible audit table, no stop.
  (Retro-records of the operator's own in-chat decisions are formal.)
- **Strategic / conceptual / taste** items → loop queue, operator stamp required.
- **Doubt escalates up, never down.**
- Provenance: nabla-lab `session/flag.md` lock 18 (extends its lock 9 delegation split).

## Two guards (gaveled as therapy-origin G-19 + G-20, bed: `raw.therapy/gavels/gavels.md`)

1. **Precondition — the loop harvests, it does not solve (G-19).** Requires processed
   substrate + an enumerated queue (cross-measures, manifests, cards — decisions made
   stampable by prior work). On raw questions the loop produces confident theatre;
   the skill must refuse or downgrade to a research recommendation.
2. **Reopen is first-class (G-20).** A closed loop reopens on one operator line
   ("maybe I caught wrong") at zero friction — protocol move, not interruption. The
   proven pattern: the session's best content came from a reopen. Seat lean has a
   compression-toward-elegance bias; the reopen is the correction organ.

## Additional mechanics (all field-proven this session)

- **Ledger duty:** every stamp appends to the project's `flag.md` (append-only,
  supersede-never-edit); consolidation is part of loop-arc close, not optional.
- **Mobile / low-bandwidth mode:** artifacts echo to chat; one-line stamps suffice;
  large emissions ask first.
- **Voice-note tolerance:** operator input arrives noisy — seat buffers, decodes,
  mirrors interpretation for confirmation BEFORE the stamp lands (never gavel a guess).
- **Arc shape:** multi-track sequencing (pick order by ripeness: enumerated queues and
  complete knowledge-states first; work-blocked gavel sets later; single-disposition
  items last).
