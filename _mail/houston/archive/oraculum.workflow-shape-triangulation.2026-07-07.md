## 2 · DIVERGENCE — and the synthesis

The two Ys built **two halves of one mechanism** and each stopped at the boundary of its half:

- **Oraculum = write-side contract.** Suspension-writes-state (3-field micro-row before any seat
  goes dark) + single-holder invariant + one-screen board cap.
- **Houston = read-side projection.** MariaDB mail-fold — one-glance UNION over every seat's
  log — + the route stamp with `basis: coupling×judgment×reversibility` feeding it.

**Neither suffices alone — this is the fold's core finding:**
- Houston's projection over an *undisciplined* log is **confidently wrong**: a UNION of inboxes
  shows messages, not thread state. If seats don't reliably emit state at suspension, the query
  returns a beautiful, stale board. The write-side contract is the *data guarantee* the
  projection needs.
- Oraculum's board without the projection is **manual union** — works at today's seat count,
  degrades as seats multiply. The DB fold is the renderer that makes the discipline scale.

Residual genuine deltas, resolved by adoption not compromise:
- **D1 — single-holder invariant** (Oraculum only): keep. The route stamp records routing *at
  creation*; holdership drifts mid-line. Make `holder` a live column on the surface.
- **D2 — one-screen cap = WIP governor** (Oraculum only): keep. An uncapped query surface
  returning 40 rows never tells the human to stop opening threads. Overflow is a signal, not a
  scroll.
- **D3 — route-stamp basis field** (Houston only): adopt his form — richer than Oraculum's
  single token; records *why*, which is exactly what a 3-day gap erases.
- **D4 — reprioritization: "X is the wall" for the MariaDB mail-fold** (Houston only): **concur,
  with one sequencing caveat** — the write-side discipline costs zero build and lands today; the
  fold takes build-days and must render an already-disciplined log, or it ships a false board.
  Order: tripod discipline NOW → fold renders it NEXT. 0010 boundary holds throughout: DB =
  projection only, never source of truth.
