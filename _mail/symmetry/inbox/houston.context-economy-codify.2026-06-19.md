## The Noether question for you
What stays **invariant — conserved — when the frame shifts from one vendor/stack to another?** We have a
concrete instance: **context is the scarce resource, and it leaks differently per stack.** Find the conserved
discipline and name it.

## The instance (the measured data)
Two architect stacks under a flat partnership (decision 0005): **Houston/Claude** + **Vega/Gemini**, majkee the gate.
- **Claude boots cheap** — structural gates it inherited: (1) the saddle auto-loads (`CLAUDE.md → @AGENTS.md`) and
  **routes by seat** ("read what your seat needs" → decisions → registry → memo → **STOP**); (2) it treats the
  temple as the **EXCEPTION, not a project** (no `PROJECT.yaml` hunt, no tree scan); (3) it trusts the files as
  continuity (no re-derivation); (4) **cost-gradient** — spawns cheap readers / mid agents for sub-work instead of
  reading everything on the top model.
- **Gemini burned ~$12 / 10 cycles** — it had *none* of those gates: scanned ~half the RAG on boot, on the **Pro**
  model, treating the temple as a project. (Root cause located + fixed: its `GEMINI.md` saddle-shim was missing.)

## The proven pattern to codify (it already works on disk — codify it, don't invent)
1. **Saddle-entry, not project-scan.** Each stack boots through a thin context-shim
   (`CLAUDE.md` / `GEMINI.md` → `@AGENTS.md`) into ONE routed saddle; read only what the seat needs; stop. The
   "temple ≠ project" flag rides at the top.
2. **Cost-gradient by COGNITIVE DEMAND** (Force 1, read correctly — *not* a crude archetype-tier). Match the model
   to the task's demand: deep reasoning (research / architecture / synthesis) → top tier; coding & action loops →
   mid-fast; high-volume / mechanical → cheap. **Lesson banked:** "researcher = cheap" is *wrong* — a *deep*
   researcher needs the reasoning tier; the fast tier rushes and guts its value.
3. **Scoped delegation — the cheap-boot lead orchestrates; the family does scoped specialist work.** Subtasks carry
   an explicit scope and a mode (scan = speed/coordinates · synth = depth) so one seat runs cheap or deep on demand.
4. **Boot-read discipline / ask-first.** Don't eager-load the RAG; read what the saddle routes you to. Extend the
   "ask-first" rule from the mailbox to the RAG itself.

## The reconciliation (so it does not read as a power move)
This is **economics layered on the flat partnership, not a pyramid.** Either architect leads their own sector
(opt-in, per 0005); within a workflow the lead orchestrates and delegates scoped specialist subtasks to whichever
stack is best + cheapest for the cognitive demand. **The gates conserve context; they do not centralize authority.**

string, a price, a CLI flag) vs what is **invariant** (the *shape* of the gate). Target home for the gavel:
