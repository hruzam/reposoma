## Position (the fork, called)
**Rigid-vs-gray is a false binary. Don't rigidify the routing — and don't treat this as a process
problem at all.** Lock ONE thin invariant, keep the rest gray ("tight invariants, loose craft"),
and recognise the pain as an **observability problem, not a routing one.**

## Reading of X
majkee's own words: the loop "is working." The stated pain is not think→task→route — it's **losing
the board across parallel seats + multi-day gaps → a complex handoff every return.** That is
state-consolidation friction. Rigidifying the routing hardens the wrong axis; it adds ceremony where
there's no failure and leaves the actual failure (lost board) untouched.

## Rigid vs gray → the minimum that must be rigid
Gray, over a minimal spine. Three invariants, nothing more:
1. **A route stamp on every drawn task-line** — `route: A(orchestrate) | B(cards-for-human) ·
   in-loop:[Janus?/Epoch?] · basis: coupling×judgment×reversibility`. Formalize the decision
   *record*, not the decision. The A/B call stays judgment.
2. **The handoff/addressing contract** — already exists (mail bus + the rolling memo). Keep it.
3. **A consolidated observability surface** — the missing invariant (see lever).
Everything else stays gray judgment: who orchestrates, when Janus/Epoch enter, the token-vs-time
dial. A/B heuristic (exposed, not hardcoded): **A** when the line is deep/coupled/canon-touching, or
short enough that re-briefing an executor costs more than doing it; **B** when tasks are
scoped/parallel/cheap/low-judgment.

## Structural lever (the non-obvious one)
**The tested subject is majkee's working memory — so score every design choice by how much it
*removes from the human's head*, not by how complete the process is.** Two cheap memory-offload
devices, no new process:
- **Finish the reposoma MariaDB mail-fold** ("project the log, not the canon") — a one-glance UNION
  of every seat's inbox. Direct antidote to "lose the board across parallel seats + gaps": it turns
  "complex handoff every return" into a single query. It was queued wait-for-the-wall — **X is the
  wall.** This reprioritizes it above the routing question entirely.
- **The route stamp feeds that surface** — routing becomes queryable, so future-majkee after 3 days
  reads how every line was routed and why, with zero re-briefing.
Net: **don't add process — subtract memory load.** The gray zone is *safe precisely because* the
observability surface catches what gray judgment drops.
